# Review a thread with /sweep

Type `/sweep` in the composer to review one decision that already has a
waiting card in that thread. Oldest comes first. Open the card to answer there,
then run `/sweep` again in the same thread. The answer on that card is the
record of progress; settled or expired cards do not come back.

The review is read-only. It does not send the command to the agent, run a tool,
read a connected inbox, grant access, or decide anything on your behalf. If an
item elsewhere needs a decision, it appears here only after it has its own
waiting card in this thread. The ordinary card still checks who can answer.
